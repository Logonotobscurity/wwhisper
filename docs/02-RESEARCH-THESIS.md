# Research Thesis

## Hypothesis
Discourse-adaptive prompting can improve contextual appropriateness when a voice agent explicitly evaluates task, audience, linguistic mode, evidence, uncertainty and discourse strategy before responding.

## Conditions
- **A — Generic:** conventional prompt.
- **B — Structured:** task + audience + objective + action close.
- **C — DAPF:** contextual assessment + strategy selection + evidence/provenance + separated uncertainty + repair.
- **D — Naive culturalization:** forced culturalized behavior regardless of contextual evidence.

## Metrics
Comprehension, interpretation quality, context sufficiency, evidence coverage, discourse alignment, action clarity, appropriateness, respect, over-culturalization, clarification quality and response length.

## Falsification
The thesis is weakened if DAPF does not outperform structured prompting on contextual cases, naive culturalization performs equally well without increased over-culturalization, or gains disappear when verbosity and clarification frequency are controlled.

Do not make causal or universal claims from a tiny convenience sample.
